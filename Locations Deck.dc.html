Sljeme — Fab City HUB

Croatia · City of Zagreb · Sesvete district
Former meat factory · 100 ha · abandoned 20 yrs
Open in Google Maps ↗
Resident-focused issue
Sesvete (~100,000 residents) would be Croatia's third-largest city, yet has no real centre and lacks essential public services — police, medical, cultural. The 100 ha former meat-factory complex sits abandoned for 20 years, fully owned by the City of Zagreb.
Expected outcome
Revitalise the complex into a Fab City HUB — a productive neighbourhood centre: a maker & innovation hub in the former HQ (3,500 m²), a music school in the storage hall (9,000 m²), and an art depot in the concrete silos — finally giving Sesvete the centre it never had.

Adriatic GreenLab

Adriatic GreenLab

Croatia · Šibenik-Knin · Prvić Luka (Vodice)
Former youth & astronomy centre · abandoned 10 yrs
Open in Google Maps ↗
Resident-focused issue
Croatia has ~1,000 islands; a century ago 100 were inhabited, now fewer than 50. A former youth & astronomy centre on Prvić has stood abandoned for 10 years while remote island communities keep shrinking.
Expected outcome
Convert it into Adriatic GreenLab — a living lab for remote areas, testing sustainable year-round island life and reversing depopulation. The proposal has been presented twice in Brussels (2019, 2022).

Grobnik Centre for Creativity

Croatia · Primorje-Gorski kotar · Grobnik (Čavle)
Former school → community centre · done 2025/26
Open in Google Maps ↗
Resident-focused issue
Small settlements lose their public buildings and footfall. A former primary school sat unused, and the area's historic fortifications attracted little economic activity.
Expected outcome
First completed transformation (2025/26): former school → Centre for creativity and science promotion. A replicable model that draws visitors to the old fortifications and creates demand for food, hospitality and broader revitalisation.

Krvavica — European Heritage Lab

Croatia · Split-Dalmatia · Krvavica (Makarska)
Former children's hospital · abandoned 30 yrs
Open in Google Maps ↗
Resident-focused issue
An architecturally significant former children's hospital has been abandoned for 30 years despite repeated bottom-up initiatives along the Makarska coast.
Expected outcome
Transform it into a European learning centre for heritage-to-innovation conversion, anchoring the Mediterranean Makers Initiative as a hub for the whole Mediterranean basin. Fablab was invited to propose in May 2026.
